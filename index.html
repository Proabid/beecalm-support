❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → BeeCalm Premium → Cancel Subscription

How do I restore my purchases?

Open BeeCalm → Paywall → Restore Purchase button

I forgot my password

BeeCalm uses iCloud sync - no password needed. Just sign into your Apple ID.
